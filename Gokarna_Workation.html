Gokarna Workation

3 Days

A 3-day workation in Gokarna, balancing work with beach days, local cafés, scooty rides, and sunsets. From meeting new people at Zostel to exploring the coastline at our own pace. Three days of work, exploration, and plenty of time by the sea.

Tour Timeline

Day 1

To Vitamin Sea

Bus

Description: Took an overnight bus from Bengaluru, checked into Zostel, met new people, worked, rented a scooty, explored Gokarna, caught sunset at Jatayu Tirtha, and danced till 2 AM.

Notes: Don’t miss the sunset at Jatayu Tirtha. It’s one of those places where the views are worth the ride.
Links: Restaurant Link

Day 2

Chasing The Coast

Scooty

Description: We rented a scooty and spent the day exploring Om Beach, grabbing breakfast at Namaste Café, and relaxing at Coco Leaf. Later, we met new people and trekked through the dark to Aloha Café.

Links: Restaurant Link

Day 3

Temples And Goodbyes

Bus

Description: We started with darshan at Mahabaleshwar Temple, followed by street shopping. Later, we rode to a quiet beach 10–15 km away and had it almost to ourselves. We ended with a sunset at Zostel before catching our bus back to Bengaluru.

Notes: Start early for temple darshan, as it can take a while.
Links: Restaurant Link
