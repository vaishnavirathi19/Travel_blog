Pin Bhaba Pass Trek

8 Days

Eight days, 1 incredible journey from the lush green valleys of Kinnaur to the rugged landscapes of Spiti, with the mighty Pin Bhaba Pass in between. Each day brought something new: long walks through forests and meadows, peaceful campsites, challenging climbs, and breathtaking mountain views. The trek tested our strength and patience, especially on the pass crossing day, but every difficult step was worth it. From early morning starts to cozy evenings under the stars, the journey was filled with little moments that made it unforgettable. Crossing the pass and watching the landscape transform before our eyes felt truly magical. Here’s a glimpse of each day of this beautiful 8 day adventure.

Tour Timeline

Day 1

The Mountain Calling

Flight - Car

Description: Flew from Bangalore to Chandigarh, then hit the road to Shimla by cab. Day 1 was all about getting to the base point, settling in, and getting ready for the adventure ahead.

Notes: Add up a meal in the flight or else you have to juggle between reaching on time or getting a food somewhere.
Links: Restaurant Link

Day 2

Towards The Himalayas

Trek

Description: The drive to Kafnu is full of beautiful mountain views, with steep cliffs on one side and deep valleys on the other. The winding roads make the journey both exciting and tiring.

Notes: Narrow, winding roads with deep gorges and possible landslides expect a bumpy ride.

Day 3

Kafnu To Mulling

Trek

Description: An 8–10 km trail with a mix of easy and challenging stretches. The trail starts through a pine forest and opens into beautiful grasslands with patches of wildflowers near Mulling.

Notes: drink enough water, take slow and steady steps.

Day 4

Mulling To Kara

Trek

Description: A 5 km easy trail with beautiful flower-filled meadows and a scenic ridge overlooking the Bhaba stream. The final 800–900 m has a steep ascent, making it the toughest stretch.

Notes: It’s a monsoon trek so be prepared for rain.

Day 5

Kara To Pushtirang

Trek

Description: A 5–6 km trek with a moderate ascent, climbing from 11,800 ft to 13,800 ft. Cross a few streams barefoot and enjoy stunning views of the Bhaba River flowing through the lush green valley.

Notes: Carry protein bars for energy.

Day 6

Acclimatisation In Pushtirang

Description: A chill day to relax and recharge before the summit push. We spent the day playing Mafia, card games, and enjoying the campsite.

Notes: Explore the beautiful campsite and enjoy the surroundings.

Day 7

To Pin Bhaba Pass

Trek

Description: A long 16–18 km trek, starting with a steep 3–4 km ascent to Pin Bhaba Pass (16,105 ft), followed by a long descent into Pin Valley to the Bwalder campsite.

Notes: Be patient with the trail as it feels never ending.

Day 8

Fairy Tale Ends

Trek - Car

Description: A 10–15 km trek to Mud Village, followed by a drive to Kaza, marking the end of our Pin Bhaba Pass trek.

Notes: Savour every bit of the last day in the mountains.
